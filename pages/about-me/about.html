A Little About Me

Hey there, I’m Allison! And I tend to follow whatever catches my interest and see where it takes me. Sometimes that turns into a new project, something I want to learn more about, or simply a fun story to tell along the way. I like having a little variety in what I do and always having something new to look forward to.


When I’m with friends and family, I’m always down for car karaoke, a late-night food run, or making a simple hangout a little more memorable. I take way too many pictures (RIP my phone storage, I might actually need 1TB), keep little things for the memories, and get way too excited when there’s something fun on my calendar. I appreciate the small moments just as much as the big ones because, really, it’s all just adding to my own lore.

What I'm Currently Up To!

Right now, I am in a season of figuring out what I want to grow into, both professionally and personally. I am focused on gaining experience in a professional setting and becoming more confident in what I bring to the table.


At the same time, I’m making more time for myself and finally giving some of my ideas a chance to become real projects. Overall, I’m working toward a routine that gives me room to grow without forgetting to enjoy everything in between.

  • Getting comfortable in professional spaces. I’m learning how I like to work with others, where I can contribute, and what kind of work I want to keep pursuing.
  • Giving my ideas somewhere to go. I have projects sitting in my notes that I’ve been wanting to make, so I’m challenging myself to actually build them instead of just saying, “that would be cool.”
  • Doing more things on my own. I want to get better at taking myself out, trying something new, or going somewhere simply because I want to, without always needing an occasion or someone to go with.
  • Making movement part of my routine. I’m working on being more consistent with working out and finding activities I genuinely enjoy enough to keep coming back to.

Why the Interest

What interests me most is the entire process of seeing an idea become a product. I like being there from the early “what if?” conversations, helping shape the direction, and eventually seeing it become something real. I don’t just want to build what’s already been decided. I want to contribute along the way and have a hand in the decisions that shape what we’re creating and why.

  • I like having a voice in the process. I enjoy contributing ideas, questioning decisions, and helping figure out which direction makes the most sense instead of only coming in once everything has already been decided.
  • Design is part of the problem solving for me. I like thinking through how someone will move through an experience and how small choices can make something clearer or easier to use.
  • I want to understand the bigger picture. Knowing why we’re building something, who it’s for, and what we want it to accomplish makes the actual building much more interesting to me.
  • Seeing it all come together is rewarding. Going from an early conversation or rough concept to something people can actually interact with is probably my favorite part of the process.

Beyond the Screen

Exploring

I’m always looking for somewhere new to explore, whether it’s a cute coffee shop or scenic point. I actually keep a running list of coffee shops and bakeries I want to try, along with parks, museums, and lookout points I want to visit.


I love slowly checking places off whenever I get the chance and adding new ones as I find them. Some of my favorite days are the ones without much of a plan, just wandering around, taking way too many pictures, and finding a new spot to add to my favorites.

Outdoor Adventures

Being outdoors gives me a chance to step away from my usual routine and do something a little more adventurous. I enjoy hiking trails with a rewarding view at the end, running along the beach, and getting out on the water to kayak or swim.

Cooking & Baking

I love cooking and baking, especially when I get to make something from scratch and realize it’s way easier than I expected. It started with things like homemade pasta sauce and has slowly turned into trying new recipes, making treats, and finding any excuse to cook for my friends and family. I also like to host little dinner parties and food tastings where everyone gets to try what I’ve been experimenting with.


The Japanese garlic bread rolls pictured here are one of my favorite examples. I didn’t have a proofing drawer, so I improvised and let them proof in the sun instead. A little unconventional, but it worked!
