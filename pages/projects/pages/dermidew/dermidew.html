01 / Overview

DermiDew

Overview Summary

Personalized skincare, without the guesswork.

DermiDew combines a camera-equipped vanity mirror with a personalized web experience to help users better understand and care for their skin.

Problem

Skincare shouldn't be trial and error.

Finding the right products and routines can be confusing, expensive, and time-consuming, while accessible skin-analysis tools provide limited personalized guidance.

Solution

Analysis that turns into action.

DermiDew transforms skin analysis into personalized concerns, routines, ingredient guidance, and product recommendations.

03 / Product Strategy

Shaping the Product

The product we ended with wasn't the product we started with.

Research clarified what users needed, while testing helped us determine how DermiDew could deliver it. Each iteration brought us closer to a product that was both practical and easy to integrate into an existing skincare routine.

01 / Product Evolution

Finding the right form.

  1. Original Concept

    Face Mask

    A hands-free wearable designed to analyze the user's skin while fitting into their routine.

    • Poor image quality up close
    • Camera sat too close to the skin

  2. First Pivot

    Wand

    A handheld direction that gave users more control over where images were captured.

    • Similar image-quality limitations
    • Required stitching facial images together

  3. Final Direction

    Vanity Mirror

    A familiar form factor that improved image capture while naturally fitting into a getting-ready routine.

    • Better camera distance
    • Natural routine integration
The best form factor wasn't the most novel one—it was the one that fit naturally into an existing behavior.

Why the Mirror?

Three signals pointed us in the same direction.

  • 01

    Better Capture

    Moving the camera farther from the face produced more usable images for analysis.

  • 02

    Natural Integration

    A mirror already belongs in the routines where users typically interact with skincare.

  • 24 / 32

    User Validated

    Most interviewees supported keeping a physical hardware component in DermiDew.

02 / Technical Challenges

Solving for a working end-to-end experience.

  1. 01

    API Integration

    Keeping API credentials secure while working with an external analysis service.

    Solution
    Environment variables kept sensitive credentials outside the codebase.
    Learned
    External services require secure credential management and thoughtful handling of latency.
  2. 02

    Dynamic UI

    Creating a responsive web experience without relying on a frontend framework.

    Solution
    JavaScript fetch() loaded modular HTML content and updated the interface dynamically.
    Learned
    Vanilla JavaScript can create SPA-like behavior while keeping the project lightweight.

06 / Reflection

Looking Back

What This Project Taught Me

The best solution wasn't the most complex one.

DermiDew began as a wearable mask, moved to a handheld scanner, and ultimately became a mirror. Each prototype showed us that adding more interaction wasn't necessarily creating a better experience.

The biggest shift was realizing that the technology should fit into an existing skincare routine rather than ask users to create a new one. That idea shaped both sides of the final product: a hands-free mirror for capture and a web experience that turns the analysis into something actionable.

If I Kept Building...

I'd focus on validating the experience beyond the MVP.

The next step would be testing DermiDew over a longer period of time—looking at whether users actually follow their recommended routines, return to track their skin, and find the recommendations useful as their skin changes.

I'd also continue refining the connection between the mirror and web app so the experience feels less like two separate tools and more like one product.

Final Takeaway

DermiDew changed the way I think about product development: the goal isn't to protect the first idea—it's to keep learning until the product fits the people you're designing for.
